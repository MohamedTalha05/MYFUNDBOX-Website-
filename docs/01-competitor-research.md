- Vendor claims (recovery rates, customer counts, analyst badges) are self-reported and unverified.

## 3. Pricing models

| Vendor | Model | Entry price |
|---|---|---|
| Paddle | % per transaction, all-inclusive | 5% + 50¢ per transaction |
| Chargebee | Fee + % of billing | Starter $0; paid plans custom |
| Recurly | Fee + % above a threshold | $249/mo + 0.9% (first $40K free) |
| Stripe Billing | % of billing volume | 0.7% of billing volume |
| Maxio | Flat, tiered by billings | $599/mo (up to $100K/mo) |
| Zoho Billing | Per organisation | $39–79/mo billed annually |
| Billsby | Flat + % above a threshold | $45/mo + 0.4% above $15K |
| **MYFUNDBOX** | **Flat, tiered by subscriber count** | **Free (25 subscribers), €199, €399/mo billed annually** |

**Takeaway:** MYFUNDBOX is the only vendor here with **flat pricing that takes no percentage of revenue** and a free plan you can use for real (live) customers. For a business billing €100K a month, that's €199 against €700 at Stripe or €849+ at Recurly. This is a strong message, but it needs confirming: the live pricing FAQ says the pricing model is "based on revenue" by default, which may contradict the flat price table.

## 5. Gaps and opportunities

**Where MYFUNDBOX can stand out (from verified or live-site capabilities)**
1. **No percentage of revenue.** Flat, predictable pricing, unlike Stripe, Paddle, Recurly, Chargebee and Billsby.
2. **Choose your own gateway.** Multiple gateways at once (Stripe, GoCardless, Mollie, PayPal, Checkout), with no lock-in to one processor, unlike Paddle and Stripe.
3. **EU-native.** Data in Frankfurt, SEPA and GoCardless direct debit, EU VAT validation, and the German accounting tools sevDesk and Lexware. Few US-built rivals cover the DACH market this well.
4. **A free plan you can run real customers on**, up to 25 subscribers.
5. **Fast set-up and SMB simplicity.** "Set up in 30 minutes", in the same spirit as Billsby's speed promise.

**Gaps compared with competitors (future roadmap, not to be claimed now)**
- Usage-based / metered billing.
- First-class SaaS metrics: MRR, ARR, churn and LTV dashboards.
- Revenue recognition (ASC 606 / IFRS 15).
- A broader public API (the brief says it covers only Plans and Pay Links today).
- Smart, ML-based retries, account updater, cancel-save flows.
- An MCP / AI-assistant interface.
- A Zapier app, if one isn't already live.

**Do not copy:** Paddle's Merchant of Record model, since MYFUNDBOX isn't a MoR. Also avoid unverified recovery percentages and analyst badges.
